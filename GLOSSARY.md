# Aquaculture Data

This project harmonizes aquaculture production information reported by international data sources for analysis across countries, years, and production categories.

## Language

**Production observation**:
A quantity or monetary value reported by a source for a country and period, under that source's production category or published total.

**EUDCF data row**:
A source-reported row from the EU aquaculture data call, identified by its year, variable, and any segment or species dimensions. Its value and unit are distinct from a production observation.
